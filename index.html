Personal Health Readonly

A private single-owner tool for personal health data viewing

About

Personal Health Readonly is a private tool that allows the owner to read their own Google Health activity, fitness, sleep, and health metrics for personal daily logs and trend analysis.

Key Points

  • All access to Google Health data is read-only; this tool does not write to Google Health
  • This is not a public service and does not offer signups
  • This website displays no actual health data
  • Retrieved data and credentials are stored privately on the owner's computer
  • Google remains the original provider of the health data

Data Handling

No health readings or credentials are sent to this public website. Data is not sold or used for advertising. The owner's assistant infrastructure processes data on the owner's behalf for requested logs and trends.
